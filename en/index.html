Historic Milestone: UASD Inaugurates the First PhD in Physics in the Dominican Republic

UASD inaugurates the first PhD in Physics in the Dominican Republic. In a historic event for national science, the UASD and MESCyT launched this pioneering program. The doctorate seeks to boost research in strategic areas such as nanotechnology, materials science, and climatology.
